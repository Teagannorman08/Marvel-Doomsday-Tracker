# 🎬 Marvel Doomsday Watch Tracker

A chronological watch-list tracker for the entire Marvel Cinematic Universe leading up to **Avengers: Doomsday** on December 18, 2026.

## Features

- 📋 **Complete MCU Watch List** - Track all movies and TV shows in chronological order
- ⏱️ **Live Countdown** - Days, hours, minutes, and seconds until Doomsday
- ✅ **Progress Tracking** - Mark individual episodes and movies as watched
- 📅 **Smart Schedule** - Auto-generated viewing schedule to finish everything by December 18, 2026
- 🔍 **Search & Filter** - Find titles by name, filter by category (MCU, Disney+, X-Men, etc.)
- 💾 **Local Storage** - Your progress is saved automatically in your browser

## Getting Started

Visit: **[www.roadtodoomsday-tn.com](https://www.roadtodoomsday-tn.com)**

## How It Works

1. Browse the complete MCU timeline from *Captain America: The First Avenger* to *Avengers: Doomsday*
2. Check off movies and episodes as you watch them
3. View the "Road to Doomsday" tab for a personalized schedule
4. Track your overall progress percentage

## Data

Every title is included in the official Marvel chronological order with verified episode counts and runtimes.
